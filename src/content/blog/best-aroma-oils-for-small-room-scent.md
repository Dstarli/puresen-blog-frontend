<p>The best aroma oil for a small room depends on the feeling you want the space to have. Some scents make a bedroom feel softer, some make a kitchen feel fresher, and some help a work corner feel clean without making medical claims.</p>

<h2>How to Choose Aroma Oils for a Room</h2>

<p>Start with the room and the moment. A small bedroom usually needs a softer scent than a large open living space. A scent for evening should feel quieter than a scent for a morning kitchen, entryway, or work corner.</p>

<h2>How Much Should You Use?</h2>

<p>Always follow the instructions for your compatible diffuser or aroma accessory first. Start small. It is easier to add more scent than to remove too much scent from a room.</p>
